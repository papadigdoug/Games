BLEVADERE CROQUET

a tournament of dubious sportsmanship & vengeful garden gnomes

How to Play

  • WASD / Arrows — move your golfer around the course
  • Mouse — aim your swing
  • Hold Left Click — charge your swing power, release to lock it in
  • Once power is locked, keep moving the mouse to fine-tune your aim before you commit
  • Click again to strike the ball
  • Right Click or Esc — cancel a shot and start over
  • Get close to the ball before you can swing at it
  • Hit every wicket in order, then finish at the stake
  • Fewer strokes = higher score. Watch the clock!
  • Evil lawn gnomes stalk the course — if your ball sits still too long near one, they'll steal it and you'll have to redo your last wicket
  • Smash a gnome by rolling your ball into it at speed — very satisfying, no penalty
  • Grab power-up icons rolling near your ball for a boost
